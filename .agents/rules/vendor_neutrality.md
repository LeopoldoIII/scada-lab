# Vendor Neutrality & Generic Enterprise Guidelines

## 1. Absolute Neutrality & Anonymity
- **Zero Company or Client Mentions:** Strictly prohibit referencing any specific corporate entity, private employer, vendor, or commercial trademark in code, comments, docstrings, filenames, test scenarios, or documentation.
- **Zero Employment or Hiring Vocabulary:** Strictly avoid terms related to hiring processes, job descriptions, interview preparation, recruitment agencies, or candidate evaluations.
- **Enterprise-Grade Generic Domain:** Use neutral, industry-standard engineering nomenclature governed by IEEE, IEC, and ISA standards:
  - *Industrial SCADA / BESS Test Automation Framework*
  - *Medium-Voltage (13.8 kV) UPS Grid Firewall*
  - *Battery Energy Storage System (BESS)*
  - *Substation Automation & NERC-CIP Compliance*
  - *Technical Architecture & Engineering Q&A*

## 2. Open-Source & Portfolio Readiness
- The repository must read as an independent, enterprise-grade open-source automation framework.
- All architectural decisions must be justified strictly through software quality engineering, OT/IT convergence standards (Purdue Model, ISA-101, IEC 62443), and resilience principles.
